# What's new

Every ON Suite release, newest first. When your server is updated to a new
version, a dot on your user menu points here until you've had a look.

## 6.5.0: ON Suite - What's new

*26 November 2026*

ON Suite now tells you when it's been updated. After an upgrade, a small
dot appears on your user menu; open **What's new** from that menu (or click
the version number at the bottom of any page) to read what changed. The dot
goes away once you've looked, on every device. Plus, connecting an assistant
is sturdier, with a few security and error-handling fixes.

## 6.4.0: ON Suite - Your assistant reads with you

*25 November 2026*

Assistants can now work with ON Reader and ON Later: catch up on your feeds, read or summarise an article, star it or mark it read, save a link to Later with tags, archive it, and tag what's there. Article text is always handed over as outside content, which makes it much harder for a web page to steer your assistant. Assistants can also set and change due dates in ON Notes.

## 6.3.0: ON Suite - Your assistant lends a hand

*25 November 2026*

Assistants can now do more than look. With your permission, they can update your ON Books reading log (start a book, record progress, finish it with a rating, add notes and quotes) and work with your ON Notes outline (search it, read a list, see what's due, add a bullet and tick it done). Nothing can be deleted, and older connections only gain these if you allowed changes when you connected.

## 6.2.0: ON Suite - Talk to your assistant

*25 November 2026*

Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini to ON Suite and ask about your books. An administrator turns AI access on, and each person chooses what their assistant may see and can disconnect it at any time. More apps will follow.

## 6.1.0: ON Notes - Made for your phone

*24 November 2026*

ON Notes is much easier to use on a phone. Nested bullets no longer
squeeze the text into a thin column: each level indents far less, and the
"..." menu moved to the end of the row, so even deep levels have room to
read. Tapping a bullet's dot to zoom in is easier too, with a bigger,
finger-sized target. Nothing changes on desktop.

## 6.0.0: ON Suite - A fresh new look

*23 November 2026*

ON Suite has a whole new look: a calm stone-and-ink palette with teal and
vermilion accents, and new type throughout, with IBM Plex for menus,
headings and numbers and Source Serif for the things you read. Every app
got attention, from a precision dial for ON Focus timers to an editorial
layout for ON Later articles. The Palette and Font choices in the user
menu are gone in favour of this one design; Light and Dark themes stay,
and ON Later keeps its own reading settings.

## 5.3.0: ON Suite - Paper and ink

*12 November 2026*

A new Ink colour palette sits beside Warm: near-black on off-white paper,
with navy accents, in both light and dark themes. Fonts are now called
Sans, Serif, Mono and Mixed. Mixed keeps menus and buttons sans and sets
the text you read in serif. Plus, scrollbars now turn dark in dark mode
in Chrome too.

## 5.2.1: ON Suite - Fresh dependencies

*11 November 2026*

A small maintenance release that brings ON Suite's libraries up to
date: Go's networking, crypto and terminal packages, the Markdown
renderer and the SQLite driver. Nothing changes in how the apps look
or work.

## 5.2.0: ON Suite - Every app in its own colour

*13 October 2026*

Each app now has a colour of its own, so you can tell them apart at a
glance. On the home page every app sits on a soft badge in its colour,
the sidebar icons match, and the app you're in is tinted in its colour
in the sidebar. It all works in light and dark, and stays calm and easy
on the eyes.

## 5.1.0: ON Books - A page for every book

*13 October 2026*

ON Books has a new look. The home screen is two panes, your shelves and
your books, and every book has a page of its own: your reading, your take,
notes, quotes and reading history, all in one place and all changed right
there. A book's details, tags and cover live on its Edit page, where Find
cover looks up what you've typed. Plus a round of fixes, and bigger touch
targets on phones.

## 5.0.0: ON Suite 5.0 - ON Books

*10 October 2026*

Meet ON Books, a private reading log: what you're reading, what you've
read, what's next and what you gave up on. Find books on Open Library or
add them by hand, track your progress page by page, rate and review them,
and keep dated notes and favourite quotes, all searchable. Set a yearly
goal and see your reading stats by year. Bring your library over from
Goodreads, with covers filled in by themselves, and download it all as
Markdown whenever you like.

## 4.1.0: ON Later - Easier reading

*9 October 2026*

Article text in ON Later is easier on the eyes: long words break with
hyphens, so the right edge is much smoother, especially in the narrow
column. If you prefer a perfectly straight edge, the new Align setting in
the Aa menu justifies the text. Later now also remembers each article's
language, so hyphenation follows the right rules and Hebrew or Arabic
articles read right to left.

## 4.0.0: ON Suite 4.0 - ON Focus

*8 October 2026*

Meet ON Focus, a calm focus timer: set up a timer once - a 15-minute
reflection, a deep-work block with breaks - and start it with one click.
It runs in a quiet full-window view with a progress ring, gentle chimes and
notifications, keeps your screen awake, and picks up where it left off if
you close the tab. History shows how much you've focused today, this week,
this month and this year. Plus, clearer text in both light and dark mode
across the suite, and a few smaller fixes.

## 3.0.0: ON Suite 3.0 - ON Later

*6 October 2026*

Meet ON Later, a read-it-later app: save any page with the bookmarklet or
the save box and read it later in a calm, distraction-free view. Highlight
passages, add comments and a note, tag and search your articles, and
download them as Markdown or export everything. Plus, ON Reader favicon
fixes and a few smaller fixes.

## 2.1.1: ON Reader - Smoother navigation

*1 October 2026*

Picking a feed, switching filters or marking everything read no longer
makes the reader flicker: only the article list is redrawn now, so your
pane widths, collapsed folders and scroll position stay put. Feeds added
by their direct feed URL also find their site's icon now.

## 2.1.0: ON Suite 2.1 - Deck export and moving feeds

*29 September 2026*

ON Flash decks can now be exported: download any deck from its toolbar to
keep a copy, share it, or import it back, and ON Flash is now part of the
full account export too. In ON Reader you can move a feed to another folder
without losing what you've read or starred, and in ON Notes you can stop
sharing a bullet right from its banner. Daily limits, streaks and stats now
follow the server's time zone instead of rolling over at midnight UTC (in
Docker, pass -e TZ=Your/Zone). Plus a round of bug fixes and polish across
every app.

## 2.0.0: ON Suite 2.0 - Admin tools and built-in help

*28 September 2026*

Admins can now manage users and run background jobs straight from the
browser, and every app has a guide built right in: open Help from the user
menu to read about the app you're in. There's a new user menu, plus a
handful of handy extras: mark older articles as read in ON Reader, copy or
export a single note in ON Notes, a more compact snippet list in ON Paste,
and better Cyrillic text. And, as always, bug fixes and polish.

## 1.1.0: ON Flash - Major UI update

*27 September 2026*

Flash cards are beautiful again! Plus, a lot of bug fixes, accessibility
and performance improvements.

## 1.0.0: ON Flash

*18 September 2026*

Meet ON Flash, flash cards that schedule themselves: make decks and cards
with tags, add images and audio, import from Markdown or JSON, share decks,
and review with a spaced-repetition engine that brings each card back just
before you'd forget it. Stats show how your reviews are going.

Releases before 1.0 were early previews, where ON Paste, ON Notes and
ON Reader first arrived.
