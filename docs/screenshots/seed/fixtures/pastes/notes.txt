Home network notes
==================

Router admin page: http://192.168.1.1 (credentials are in the password manager)
ISP support line: on the sticker under the modem

Networks
- Home        5 GHz + 2.4 GHz, band steering on
- Home-IoT    2.4 GHz only, for the smart plugs and the robot vacuum
- Home-Guest  isolated, 20 Mbps cap, password changes every quarter

Fixed addresses
- 192.168.1.10  NAS
- 192.168.1.20  Raspberry Pi (ON Suite, Pi-hole)
- 192.168.1.30  printer in the study

If the internet drops
1. Check the modem lights: DSL should be solid, not blinking.
2. Power-cycle the modem, wait two minutes, then the router.
3. Still down? Check the ISP status page from a phone on mobile data.
