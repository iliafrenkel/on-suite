Small software is software one person can hold in their head. It does a few things, does them well, and can be read in an afternoon.

It is easy to underrate. Small tools rarely look impressive in a demo, and they do not grow a team around them. But they are the ones still running ten years later, because nobody was ever afraid to change them.

Three habits

  1. Say no to the feature that only one person wants once.
  2. Keep one way of doing each thing.
  3. Write down why, not just what.

None of these are clever. They are a kind of patience with your own code: choosing to leave it smaller than you could make it.
